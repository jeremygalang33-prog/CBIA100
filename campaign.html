ACTIVITY 03 • MARKETING CAMPAIGN

#BiteIntoBreak

Turn your short school break into a tasty one with Campus Bites.

Join the Campaign

THE IDEA

A quick break deserves a good bite.

The campaign targets students and young adults who want affordable, tasty, and convenient food. The campaign uses social media-friendly content, simple offers, and a clear message.

STUDENT DEAL 10% OFF

Show your student ID and enjoy the deal.

CAMPAIGN PLAN

Reach. Engage. Convert.

01 • Reach

Post short videos and food photos on TikTok, Facebook, and Instagram.

02 • Engage

Use polls, student-friendly posts, and the hashtag #BiteIntoBreak.

03 • Convert

Offer a limited student discount to encourage first-time purchases.
